## 💡 Quick Tips for a Great Recording

1. **Speak Naturally**: Talk like you're explaining a cool project to a smart friend. Keep your tone friendly and confident.
2. **Press F11**: Full-screen mode makes your dashboard look clean and professional without any browser tabs showing.
3. **Reset First**: Click **Reset** before you record so all numbers start at `0`. When you click **Run Attack Simulation**, viewers will love watching the numbers jump!
4. **Pause on Traps**: When you mention the fake payroll file or fake password, hold your mouse still over that icon for 1 or 2 seconds so people can see it clearly.
